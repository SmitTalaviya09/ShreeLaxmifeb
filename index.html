Who We Are

A New Unit, Built Right From Day One

Shree Laxmifeb was commissioned in 2026 in Surat — the heart of India's synthetic textile industry. We are new, and we treat that as our advantage: every machine on our floor is current generation, every process was designed rather than inherited, and every buyer we take on still gets the owner on the phone.

We weave filament polyester on water jet looms and supply greige fabric to processors, traders and garment houses across India, with export documentation handled in-house.
